📋 Vehicle Inspection SOP

📝 How to Complete This Inspection

  1. Select the current week number from the grid
  2. Select the vehicle you are inspecting
  3. Select your name from the dropdown
  4. Record the previous and current mileage from the odometer
  5. Check all fluid levels and vehicle condition items
  6. Report any defects immediately to your supervisor
  7. Hit Submit when complete
✅ This form must be completed at the start of each week before the vehicle is used.

🔧 Tools Required

  • Clean cloth or paper towel for dipstick checks
  • Tire pressure gauge
  • Flashlight (for undercarriage inspection)
  • Gloves (recommended when checking fluids)

⚗️ Fluids & Chemicals

  • Engine Oil — SAE 5W-30 — Check level only. Do not add without supervisor approval.
  • Coolant/Antifreeze — OAT Orange Coolant — Check level only. Do not add without supervisor approval.
  • Windshield Washer Fluid — Stored in Maintenance Shop by back entrance
  • Fuel — Regular Unleaded — See fueling procedure below
⚠️ Always wear gloves when handling fluids. Dispose of used fluids properly — do not pour down drains.

⛽ Fueling Procedure

  1. Notify a maintenance tech or supervisor — they must be present with the key to engage the fuel switch
  2. Drive to the back of the Zoo entrance
  3. Stop at the first gate — open it and drive through
  4. Get out and LOCK the gate behind you — must be fully locked, not just closed or ghost locked. Check it!
  5. Proceed to the next gate — repeat the same process
  6. Only proceed to the fuel tank once ALL gates are locked behind you
  7. Supervisor/tech engages the keyed fuel switch
  8. Fuel with regular unleaded only
  9. When finished reverse the process — lock all gates behind you on the way out
  10. Return key to supervisor immediately
⚠️ CRITICAL — Gates must be LOCKED behind you at all times. Not just closed. Ghost locking is NOT acceptable. An unlocked gate creates a dangerous situation where customers could access restricted areas. If you are unsure if a gate is properly locked — CHECK IT AGAIN!
